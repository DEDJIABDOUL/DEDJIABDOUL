**Genomics | Bioinformatics | Scientific Software**

I build open tools for researchers working with biological data, and I use computation to study diseases that matter for Africa.

My background is in health sciences, and my work now centres on genomics and bioinformatics. I design and maintain software that makes analysis faster, easier to reproduce and accessible to more people.

## Vision

Many endemic diseases in Africa are still poorly understood at the molecular level, and African populations are under-represented in genomic research.

My long-term goal is to use **omics sciences** to understand these diseases better: how pathogens evolve, how hosts respond and what makes local populations vulnerable or resistant. I also want to build the computational tools that make this research possible in African settings.
