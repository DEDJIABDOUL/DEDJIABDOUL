## My Perspective

Biological data is becoming increasingly abundant.

Genomes, transcriptomes, clinical datasets and high-throughput sequencing technologies are generating information at a scale that traditional approaches cannot fully exploit.

But having data is not enough.

The real challenge is knowing **what question to ask, which computational strategy to use, how to validate the result and, most importantly, how to translate computation back into biology.**

This is the kind of problem I want to solve.

**Biology gives me the question.
Computation gives me the method.
Data gives me the evidence.
Scientific reasoning gives meaning to the result.**
