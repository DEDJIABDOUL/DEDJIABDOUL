## Research Approach

I believe effective computational biology requires more than the ability to execute bioinformatics software.

A rigorous analysis should begin with a clearly defined biological question, use an appropriate computational method, consider the characteristics and limitations of the underlying data, apply suitable statistical reasoning, and conclude with a biologically meaningful interpretation.

For this reason, I aim to develop workflows that integrate biological knowledge, computational methods, statistics, software engineering, documentation, and reproducibility.

---

## Reproducible Research

Reproducibility is a central principle of my computational practice.

I use version control, structured project organization, documented computational environments, workflow automation, and explicit methodological descriptions whenever appropriate.

My goal is to make analytical work sufficiently transparent that another researcher can understand the methodology, reproduce the computational steps, evaluate the results, and build upon the work.
